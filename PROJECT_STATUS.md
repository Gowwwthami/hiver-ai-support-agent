## 6. Known limitations / honest caveats

- **Intent boundary confusion is the dominant failure mode**: macro F1 ≈ 0.51 and
  false-AUTO-handle 0.275 both trace to `complaint_or_human_assistance` /
  `information_or_policy` / `booking_change_or_cancellation` confusions. The `weak.py`
  bucket-based supervision cannot separate them well. Reviewed gold labels are the
  fix path (Section 4) plus the boundary rules proposed in `TOP_5_FAILURES.md`.
- **Offline judge is a deterministic heuristic proxy**, not a real LLM. Judge↔human
  *response-quality* agreement is not computable (human finals are label decisions,
  not response ratings; see README).
- **Human review is single-reviewer and non-independent.** It is recorded by the
  candidate; IAA is not claimed. The gold set itself was not modified by the review.
- **Acknowledgment-path drafts are templated** (33 ack examples) and reuse brand
  templates note-for-note; they inflate completeness but not correctness.
- Legal/security markers now run globally before intent branches (BA_351856 lawsuit
  correctly routes ESCALATE). Remaining false-auto cases are non-legal boundary
  confusions above. Evidence-sufficiency gating remains per-intent-set.

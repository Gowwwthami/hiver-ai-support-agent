## 6. Known limitations / honest caveats

- **Intent boundary confusion is the dominant failure mode**: macro F1 0.477,
  false-AUTO-handle 0.25, and four of the five top failures all trace to
  `complaint_or_human_assistance` / `information_or_policy` /
  `booking_change_or_cancellation` confusions. The `weak.py` bucket-based
  supervision cannot separate them well. Boundary rules are proposed in
  `TOP_5_FAILURES.md`.
- **Offline judge is a deterministic heuristic proxy**, not a real LLM.
  Judge↔human *response-quality* agreement is not computable (human finals are
  label decisions, not response ratings; see README).
- **Human review is single-reviewer and non-independent.** It is recorded and
  applied by the candidate; IAA is not claimed. The pre-review gold remains
  archived (`_labels.tsv`, `golden_set_recommendations.pre_human_review_backup.csv`)
  so metrics are re-derivable against either label set.
- **Acknowledgment-path drafts are templated** (non-support examples) and reuse
  brand templates note-for-note; they inflate completeness but not correctness.
- **2017 archive, not current policy.** Historical BA replies are evidence, not
  ground truth.
- Legal/security markers now run globally before intent branches (BA_351856
  lawsuit correctly routes ESCALATE). Remaining false-auto cases are non-legal
  boundary confusions above. Evidence-sufficiency gating remains per-intent-set.
